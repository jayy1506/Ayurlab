> **Official Laboratory Manual for BAMS Scholars & Faculty Evaluators**

---

## How to Perform Experiments in the Virtual Laboratory

1. **Select the Proper Utensil**: Go to the **Utensils** tab in the Inventory drawer. Drag and drop the recommended utensil onto the Canvas workspace.
2. **Add Ingredients in Sequence**: Drag each required ingredient onto the central bowl. When the **Measurement Modal** appears, enter the exact amount specified in the table.
3. **Perform Laboratory Operations**: Click the corresponding action buttons from the **Actions Panel** (e.g. *Grind/Mardana*, *Sieve/Vastragalita*, *Heat/Agni Paka*, *Filter/Galana*, *Mix*, or *Form Pills/Vati Kalpana*).
4. **Receive Product & Faculty Evaluation**: Upon successful execution, the final Ayurvedic formulation transforms dynamically with its batch certificate, yield, and evaluation report!
